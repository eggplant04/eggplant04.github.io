About Me

Hi, I’m Josh, a Game Programmer specialising in Unreal Engine 5 and C++ development. I have a strong foundation in gameplay and systems programming, with a particular interest in multiplayer, VR, and physics-based gameplay. I enjoy solving complex technical problems and working alongside designers and artists to turn ideas into polished, playable experiences.

That willingness to learn has been a major part of how I’ve developed as a programmer. When I wanted to understand multiplayer development, I set myself the goal of learning it properly and began applying Unreal Engine’s networking systems to increasingly complex gameplay. That journey eventually led to helping build a multiplayer game that was selected to be showcased at PAX Australia.

VR is another area I’m particularly passionate about. I developed Sanders Archers, a VR archery game where I worked on physical bow and arrow interactions, including two-handed aiming, drawing and releasing arrows, draw-strength-based projectile physics, object grabbing, and haptic feedback. Building it showed me how much I enjoy the unique challenges of making interactions feel natural and responsive in VR, and I’d love to continue exploring VR professionally, particularly where it intersects with physics and multiplayer systems.

Want to see more? Explore the projects mentioned above, along with my other work, on the Projects page.
